## 🟠 脆弱性情報

    - **概要**: A critical Metabase SQL injection vulnerability was exploited in zero-day attacks, impacting customer instances and allowing data theft.
    - **CVSS**: 10.0
    - **対応**: Metabaseは攻撃に使用されたエンドポイントをブロックし、修正をロールアウトした。
- **Metabase Zero-Day Exploited in Wild Allows Admin Access Without Authentication** (https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/metabase-zero-day-exploited-in-wild.html)
    - **概要**: A maximum-severity security flaw in Metabase has been exploited in the wild as a zero-day, allowing unauthenticated remote attackers to gain administrator access.
    - **CVSS**: 10.0
    - **対応**: Metabase Cloudインスタンスは更新済み。セルフホスト版はパッチ適用を推奨。一時的な回避策として「/api/session/reset_password」エンドポイントのブロックが推奨されている。

    - **CVSS**: 8.2 (CVE-2026-18577), 8.2 (CVE-2026-18556)
    - **対応**: N-central Hotfix 2の適用。
- **Progress Kemp LoadMaster Flaw Hits CISA KEV After 792 Reported Exploit Attempts** (https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/progress-kemp-loadmaster-flaw-hits-cisa.html)
    - **概要**: A critical command injection vulnerability in Progress Kemp LoadMaster, tracked as CVE-2026-8037, has been added to the CISA KEV catalog due to active exploitation attempts.
